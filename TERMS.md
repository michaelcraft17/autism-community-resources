# Terms of Use

This site publishes a curated, compiled directory of autism and disability
community resources: verified names, addresses, and service details drawn
from public sources, cross-checked, geocoded, deduplicated, and organized
by our own editorial process.

## What's freely allowed

- Browsing, searching, and using the site to find services for yourself,
  your family, or someone you support.
- Using the built-in "Export CSV" feature to save your own individual
  search results for personal, offline reference.
- Linking to this site or citing individual listings with attribution.

## What's not permitted without prior written permission

- Automated scraping, crawling, or bulk downloading of this site or its
  underlying data files (including `community_data.js` and
  `community_resources.json`).
- Reproducing, redistributing, republishing, or reselling this compiled
  database (in whole or in substantial part), including for use in
  training machine learning or AI models.
- Mirroring this dataset on another site or service.

## Why

The individual facts in this directory (an organization's name, address,
or phone number) are drawn from public sources and aren't owned by anyone.
What *is* ours is the compilation: the specific selection, verification,
geocoding, deduplication, and categorization work that turns scattered
public records into a usable, accurate directory. That compilation is
protected, and copying it wholesale undermines the work that keeps it
accurate and undercuts the reason this resource is offered for free.

If you'd like to use this data for research, a nonprofit project, or
something that benefits the autism/disability community, reach out — we're
generally happy to work something out. Automated bulk copying without
asking is not.

## Enforcement

Automated tools that identify themselves via User-Agent are asked to
respect `robots.txt`. This document establishes the terms under which the
site is offered; violating it may result in access being blocked and,
where applicable, other legal remedies.
